# T-shirt and polo garment parts and standard print/embroidery placement areas

Note: some search-tool summaries (not full-page reads) were used for items marked "(search summary)". Many results were low-quality mirror/template pages and were excluded. Dimensions vary by source; all are starting points.

## 1. Full list of tee/polo components (colourable / decoratable parts)

### Takeaway
A polo adds collar, collar stand, placket, buttons, cuffs/ribs and side vents to a tee's body, sleeves, neck rib and hem. Tee-specific decoration zones come from print shops, while polo structural parts come from sourcing/manufacturer listings. I found no single authoritative tee/polo anatomy source; the one anatomy page fetched covers dress shirts only.

### Cited Findings
- Dress-shirt anatomy (analogous, not tee/polo): collar, button placket, buttons, pocket (usually left chest patch), sleeve, cuff, sleeve placket, underarm gusset, back yoke (panel across upper back), tail/hem, sweep, seams (flat-felled vs serged) — [SewGuide](https://sewguide.com/parts-of-a-shirt/)
- Polo components: collar, collar stand, placket, buttons, sleeve cuff or rib, chest, shoulder, body length, side slit, bottom hem — (search summary) [polo POM guide result list](https://ae.linkedin.com/in/arun-k-a4347922)
- Polo collar options: flat knit, ribbed, self-fabric; collars/cuffs often have contrast tipping (single or double stripe); contrast stitching and contrast overlock seams appear in listings — (search summary) [Corporate Casuals](https://www.corporatecasuals.com/embroidered/polo-shirts/page13), [Kustom Kit tipped polo](https://www.safetydirect.ie/kustom-kit-tipped-collar-polo.html), [Asquith & Fox tipped polo](https://www.a4apparel.co.uk/polo-shirts/ladies-polo-shirts/ladies-contrast-polo-shirts/asquith-fox-womens-classic-fit-tipped-polo.html)
- Buttons: dyed-to-match or contrast (white/pearlized); placket 2- or 3-button, or zip — (search summary) [Corporate Casuals](https://www.corporatecasuals.com/embroidered/polo-shirts/page13), [Print Simple](https://www.printsimple.eu/en/clothing/polo-shirts/elevate-liberty-polo-shirt)
- Labels: inside neck label (printable) and outside label are separate print areas; heat-transfer/tagless labels and contrast neck tape appear on polo listings — [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide), (search summary) [Corporate Casuals](https://www.corporatecasuals.com/embroidered/polo-shirts/page13)
- Back yoke on polos is an embroidery/print zone ("upper back / back collar") about 1 in below the collar seam, small logos 1-3 in wide — (search summary) [Rush Order Tees](https://www.rushordertees.com/blog/logo-placement-business-clothing/)

### Inferences
- Candidate part list for a studio: front body, back body, left/right sleeve (plus sleeve hem band/cuff rib on long sleeve and polo), collar (neck rib on tee; fold-over collar + collar stand on polo), placket + buttons (polo only), chest pocket (if offered), side seams/vents, bottom hem, shoulder seams/yoke, nape/back neck, inside neck label, outside label, contrast stitching/tipping.
- Coloured-by-customer in typical decorator catalogs: body colour (blank choice), print/embroidery artwork. Tipping, button colour, stitching, neck tape are blank-manufacturer attributes (inferred from listings showing them as fixed SKU features, not decorator options).

### Gaps
- No authoritative tee/polo-specific anatomy glossary found; no standard dimensions for collar rib, placket length, or cuff band.
- Which parts are customer-selectable vs manufacturer-only is not stated by any source; only inferable from retail/blank listings.

## 2. Standard print and embroidery placements and sizes

### Takeaway
Core zones: left chest, center chest, full front, full back, upper back, nape/back collar, sleeve, inside/outside label, pocket. Sources agree on zone names but differ on exact sizes and offsets; hem print is poorly covered.

### Cited Findings
Printful (DTG/print; embroidery) — [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide):
- Print left chest: 2.5x2.5 to 5x5 in; about 3 in below neckline, 2 in from armpit.
- Print center chest: 6x6 to 10x8 in (8x8 most common); 3-3.5 in below collar.
- Print full front: 12x16 in most products; 15x18 in on select DTG products/sizes; 3-4 in below collar.
- Embroidery left chest and center chest: max 4x4 in; large center embroidery 10x6 in.
- Back (DTG): 10x12 to 12x16 in depending on product; upper back sits between shoulder blades (no distance given).
- Sleeve: usually 4x3.5 in (AOP up to ~20x12 in).
- Inside label: most 3x3 in (some 2x2 or 2.5x2.5); max logo on inside label 3x1.13 in. Outside label: 3x3 in, extra $2.49 per garment, not on racerbacks.

ScreenPrinting.com (screen print, adult tee) — [ScreenPrinting.com](https://www.screenprinting.com/blogs/news/t-shirt-design-placement-guide-your-easy-reference-for-industry-standard-layouts):
- Left chest 3.5x2 in (up to 4.5 wide), graphic centre 2.5-3 in below collar, 3.5-4 in from centre.
- Center chest 8.5x4 in, 2.5-3 in below collar. Full front 11x11 in (up to 12 wide; 11-16 tall), 3 in below collar.
- Short sleeve 2.5x1 in, ~1 in above sleeve hem. Long sleeve up to 2.5 wide x 14 long, stopping 3.5 in above cuff (page's own text and table are internally inconsistent).
- Back collar/nape 2.5x2.5 in, 1-2 in below rear collar seam. Upper back 12x4 in, 4 in below rear collar. Full back 12x14 in (to 13x16), 3-4 in below collar.
- Neck label (tag) print 2x2 to 3x3 in, after removing manufacturer tag.
- Youth: scale down 15-20%, start 2-2.5 in from collar. XXL+: scale up ~15%, lower to 2.75-3.75 in.
- No hem or pocket guidance on the page.

Embroidery Library (embroidery) — [emblibrary](https://emblibrary.com/learn/how-to/embroidery-placement-guide):
- Polo/button-down and tee left chest (no pocket): 7-9 in down from left shoulder seam; polo centred between placket and side seam (or 4-6 in right of placket); tee between centre and side seam.
- With pocket: centred above or on the pocket. Shirt front (centred designs): top 3.5-4.5 in below neck. Shirt back: 5 in below collar. Cuff monogram: 1 3/8 in right of buttonhole, 1/4 in above cuff edge.

Polo-specific (search summary, vendors vary): left chest logo 3-4 in wide, typically 7.5-9 in below shoulder seam; women's polos 5-7 in down from shoulder/collar junction; logo bottom no lower than bottom of placket; upper back/yoke 1 in below collar seam, 1-3 in wide; hoop sizes 15/18 cm for left chest, 21/24 cm for full chest/back/yoke — [Rush Order Tees](https://www.rushordertees.com/blog/logo-placement-business-clothing/), [Elite Promo](https://www.shopelitepromo.com/blog/polo-shirt-logo-placement-guide), [Garment Printer Ink](https://www.garmentprinterink.com/blogs/embroidery-machine-repair-articles/embroidery-placement-hooping-for-polo-shirts)

Pocket and hem (search summary):
- Blackwater Studios (UK): breast/pocket max about 9x10 cm; measured from bottom of neck hem; chest print 5 cm and breast pocket 7.5 cm below neck hem on adult crew; hem art is anchored to bottom hem — [Blackwater Studios](https://blackwaterstudios.co.uk/resources/print-area-templates)
- Alphabroder blank-garment spec: chest/pocket zone 3.5x3.5 in; chest-pocket 2.75x3.5 in; "left chest above pocket" 3.5x3.5 in — [Alphabroder](https://www.alphabroder.com/static/pdf/Screen%20Printing%20Locations+Colors_April2023.pdf)
- Printing just above the pocket allows slightly larger art — [Rush Order Tees pocket guide](https://www.rushordertees.com/blog/pocket-printing-guide/)
- Sleeve print ~1 in above hem (adults) — [Totally Promotional](https://www.totallypromotional.com/blog/?p=26675)

Disagreements:
- Left chest print size: Printful 2.5-5 in square vs ScreenPrinting.com 3.5x2 in; vertical offset: Printful ~3 in below neckline vs ScreenPrinting 2.5-3 in to graphic centre vs emblibrary 7-9 in below shoulder seam (different reference points).
- Back neck/nape: ScreenPrinting 2.5x2.5 in; another source 3x1.5 in; a UK printer 2.5 cm (search summary).
- Full back start: 3-4 in below collar (ScreenPrinting) vs 5 in (emblibrary).
- Sleeve: Printful 4x3.5 in vs ScreenPrinting 2.5x1 in.
- Inside label: Printful max 3x1.13 in vs ScreenPrinting 2x2 to 3x3 in.

### Inferences
- A studio model could define zones as: front_center, left_chest, right_chest, pocket, back_full, back_upper/yoke, nape, left_sleeve, right_sleeve, hem, inside_label, outside_label, with polo-specific placket exclusion.
- Hem prints and right chest are niche; only vendor-specific sources cover them.

### Gaps
- No reliable dimensioned spec for body-hem (bottom) print; sources mention only sleeve hem.
- No dimensioned right-chest spec beyond a mirror statement (Rush Order Tees, search summary).
- Shoulder print and DTG Gooten/ColorMePrinting/ShirtSpace pages not fetched.

## 3. Customer-offered vs manufacturer-only areas

### Takeaway
Print-on-demand platforms offer decoration at body/sleeve/label zones; construction attributes (tipping, stitching colour, buttons, neck tape) appear as fixed attributes of the blank. This is mostly inference.

### Cited Findings
- Printful offers inside label and outside label as paid/optional print placements; seam-to-seam AOP for full wrap — [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide)
- Contrast tipping, contrast stitching, button colour, branded interlinings and tagless/heat-transfer labels appear in custom polo manufacturer offerings — (search summary) [Corporate Casuals](https://www.corporatecasuals.com/embroidered/polo-shirts/page13), [LSLT manufacturer](https://www.lslt-shirt.com/polo-shirt-categories)
- 4imprint FAQ exists on custom polos (not read) — [4imprint](https://www.4imprint.com/blog/faq/custom-polo-shirts)

### Inferences
- Likely customer-facing: body colour, front/back/sleeve/chest/pocket/nape/label artwork. Likely manufacturer-facing (MOQ/custom-cut-and-sew): collar/cuff rib colour, tipping, button colour, stitching colour, placket style, neck tape.

### Gaps
- No source states this split explicitly; manufacturer MOQs and which attributes are customizable were not verified.

## 4. Left vs right chest convention

### Takeaway
"Left chest" means the wearer's left, which appears on the viewer's right when facing the garment.

### Cited Findings
- Industry standard is wearer's left; right chest is occasional — (search summary) [Lawson Screen Printing](https://lawsonsp.com/screen-printing-left-chest), [Rush Order Tees](https://www.rushordertees.com/blog/logo-placement-business-clothing/)
- Polo: logo sits between placket edge and side seam; placket removes centre-chest option — (search summary) [Rush Order Tees](https://www.rushordertees.com/blog/logo-placement-business-clothing/)
- Printful and ScreenPrinting.com pages do not state which side "left" means — [Printful](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide), [ScreenPrinting.com](https://www.screenprinting.com/blogs/news/t-shirt-design-placement-guide-your-easy-reference-for-industry-standard-layouts); emblibrary also silent — [emblibrary](https://emblibrary.com/learn/how-to/embroidery-placement-guide)
- Pocket tee: left chest with pocket goes on/above pocket — [emblibrary](https://emblibrary.com/learn/how-to/embroidery-placement-guide)

### Inferences
- In a front-view canvas, left chest should render on the right-hand side of the image; back view mirrors (wearer's left appears on viewer's left).

### Gaps
- Convention confirmed only via search summaries of print-shop blogs, not a formal standard.
