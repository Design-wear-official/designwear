# Competitor T-shirt/polo customization areas and options (Printful, Printify, Custom Ink, Spreadshirt, Bonfire)

Research date: 2026-11-08. Several official help pages returned HTTP 403 (Printful help centre, Spreadshirt help, Printify help); some facts come from search-result summaries of those pages and are flagged. Note: WebFetch/WebSearch return model-summarised text, not raw pages.

## Which print placements does each tool offer (tee and polo), and max dimensions?

### Takeaway
The de-facto industry set is: left chest, centre chest, full front (plus oversized front), back (collar/upper/full), sleeves (left/right), inside neck label, outside neck label; pocket is a sub-case of left chest. Hem, side seam and back yoke are not documented by any of the tools researched. Max sizes are near-identical for Printful and Printify (they share the same garment blanks/providers).

### Cited Findings
- Printful (blog guide, table): left chest print 2.5x2.5 to 5x5 in; left chest embroidery 4x4 in; centre chest print 6x6 to 10x8 in (8x8 most common); centre chest embroidery 4x4 in; full front 12x16 in standard, 15x18 in on select DTG products; large front embroidery 10x6 in; back (DTG) 10x12 to 12x16 in by product; sleeve usually 4x3.5 in (up to ~20x12 in on all-over-print); inside label usually 3x3 in (some 2x2 or 2.5x2.5; logo max 3x1.13 in); outside label 3x3 in for most garments; seam-to-seam all-over print has no standard max. Pocket, hem, side not covered. — [Printful placement guide](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide)
- Printful custom embroidered shirts: placements listed are left chest, centre chest, left sleeve, both sleeves, and on some shirts a large centre placement. Large front embroidery is not available on every tee; check the "File guidelines" tab per product. — [Printful embroidered shirts](https://www.printful.com/ca/custom-embroidered-shirts) (via search summary), [Printful placement guide](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide)
- Printful custom inside labels: inside-neck label print max area 3x3 in, $0.99 per label. — [Printful inside labels](https://www.printful.com/ca/custom-inside-labels) (via search summary)
- Printful front area upgraded from 12x16 to 15x18 in for select DTG products/sizes. — [Printful placement guide](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide)
- Printify (blog guide): centre chest 6x6 to 10x8 in (8x8 common); left chest 2.5x2.5 to 5x5 (4x4 standard), ~3 in below shoulder seam; full front 10-12 w x 10-14 h in; oversized front 12-15 x 14-16 in (provider-dependent); back: collar/small upper back 1-3 in, upper back 10-14 x 1-6 in, full back 10-14 x 6-15 in; sleeve 1-4 in (up to 20x12 on all-over-print); pocket 2.5-4 in, sits on left chest. Neck labels, hems, sides not covered in that guide. — [Printify placement guide](https://printify.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide/)
- Printify: inner neck label 2.5x2.5 in, smallest design area; some providers support front areas up to 15x18 in on larger adult tees. Not all providers support sleeve printing; print areas differ per provider. — [Printify t-shirt design size](https://printify.com/blog/t-shirt-design-size) (via search summary), [Printify placement guide](https://printify.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide/)
- Printify help centre says you can add a neck label (inner or outer) and/or a sleeve print when designing a t-shirt or sweatshirt. — [Printify custom neck label help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/9691640023185-How-can-I-create-a-custom-neck-label) (via search summary)
- Printify catalogue has a dedicated "Unisex Pocket T-shirt" (Bella+Canvas) and an embroidery polo (Port Authority). — [Pocket tee](https://printify.com/app/products/1358/bella-canvas/unisex-pocket-t-shirt), [Embroidery polo](https://printify.com/app/products/1730/port-authority/unisex-polo-shirt-embroidery)
- Printify per-product example (third party catalogue, Bella 3001): sleeves 1181x1181 px, inner neck label 750x750 px; Gildan 64000 front 4500x5100 px (15x17 ratio). Varies by provider. — [mydesigns.io Bella 3001](https://mydesigns.io/product-catalog/t-shirts/product/bella-3001), [mydesigns.io Gildan 64000](https://mydesigns.io/product-catalog/t-shirts/product/gildan-64000)
- Custom Ink Design Lab: Front/Back toggle buttons (top right); a "Sleeve Print" control beneath them opens special print areas "such as sleeve, hood, and side of leg (when applicable)" with pricing; "Add Names" for name/number personalisation. Availability of areas varies by style. — [Custom Ink: navigate the lab](https://www.customink.com/help_center/navigate-the-lab), [Custom Ink FAQ](https://www.customink.com/custom/custom-clothing)
- Custom Ink FAQ says print on front, back, pocket and sleeves. Polo shirts are sold in embroidered, printed, performance and golf styles. No polo-specific print-zone sizes found. — [Custom Ink FAQ](https://www.customink.com/custom/custom-clothing)
- Spreadshirt: official help gives only generic info: a "print area" is the region where printing is possible, shown in the Design Tool as a black/white square; an aid line shows when design is centred; a T-shirt design at 200 dpi can be up to 4000x4000 px. No official per-placement sizes or sleeve/label positions found. — [Spreadshirt Customize Tool help](https://help.spreadshirt.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000052513-Help-with-the-Customize-Tool) (via search summary)
- Printful polo page: polos are embroidery only (no printed polos); 15 thread colours, up to 6 per design file; digitisation fee £4.95/file (UK page). Placements not stated on that page. — [Printful UK custom polos](https://www.printful.com/uk/custom-polo-shirts)
- Polo convention: left chest is the standard logo position; sleeve is a secondary discreet spot; placket occupies centre so left-chest logo ~3-3.5 in wide. — [Printify placement guide](https://printify.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide/) (left chest/sleeve polo notes); placket/3-3.5 in claim from a third-party guide: [Rush Order Tees](https://www.rushordertees.com/blog/logo-placement-business-clothing/) (via search summary, weak)

### Inferences
- Printful and Printify likely share identical max sizes because they draw on the same blanks (Bella+Canvas, Gildan) -- inference from near-identical tables.
- A "standard" placement set for a studio: front-centre, left chest (also hosts pocket), right chest (not explicitly documented by any tool; symmetric inference), back (collar/upper/full), left+right sleeve, inside neck label, outside neck label. Custom Ink is the only one noted offering unusual areas (hood, side of leg) but those are not tee placements.

### Gaps
- No source documented hem, side-seam or back-yoke printing for any tool (Printful/Printify guides explicitly omit them). Probably not offered as standard.
- Right-chest placement not explicitly confirmed anywhere.
- Spreadshirt per-placement sizes and sleeve/neck-label positions not found; Custom Ink print-area sizes not found. Printful back-print official page and Printful polo placement list not found.
- Pocket placement is described only as "within the pocket boundary" by Printify; no max found for Printful.

## Can users recolour parts separately (collar, sleeves, cuffs, pocket, buttons, body)? Polo button colour?

### Takeaway
No evidence that any researched tool lets customers recolour individual garment parts or choose polo button colour; colour is a single per-variant garment colour chosen from the blank's fixed colourways. Contrast-trim products (raglan/baseball) exist as pre-defined colourways rather than per-part pickers.

### Cited Findings
- Custom Ink Design Lab exposes "Product Colors" (changes the colour of the current product) and "Add Products" for extra styles/colours; no per-part colour setting or 3D/preview mode mentioned. — [Custom Ink: navigate the lab](https://www.customink.com/help_center/navigate-the-lab)
- Printify embroidered polo listing describes flat knit collar/cuffs and metal buttons with dyed-to-match plastic rims, i.e. fixed by the blank. — [Printify embroidery polo](https://printify.com/app/products/1730/port-authority/unisex-polo-shirt-embroidery) (via search summary)
- Printful: nothing found indicating collar/cuff/button colour choice; colour choices stated are garment colours and 15 embroidery thread colours. — [Printful UK custom polos](https://www.printful.com/uk/custom-polo-shirts)
- Bonfire: shirt colour and ink colours are chosen after uploading/creating art; max 8 ink colours in the design tool; tool tee images are meant to match real life. Offers a 3/4-sleeve baseball (raglan) tee and a pocket tee as catalogue products. — [Bonfire blog](https://blog.bonfire.com/?p=19), [Bonfire design tool basics](https://help.bonfire.com/en/articles/2185230-design-tool-basics), [Bonfire baseball tee](https://bonfire.com/catalog/3-4-sleeve-baseball-t-shirt), [Bonfire pocket tee](https://www.bonfire.com/catalog/comfort-colors-long-sleeve-pocket-tee/)
- Bonfire also lists an "embroidered premium polo" in its catalogue. — [Bonfire polo](https://bonfire.com/catalog/embroidered-premium-polo)

### Inferences
- Contrast sleeve/collar looks are sold as separate SKUs/colourways (e.g. baseball tee) rather than a part-recolour tool -- inference; Bonfire's baseball tee swatches were not verified.
- Offering per-part colour (collar, cuffs, placket, buttons, pocket) in DesignWear would be a differentiator versus these tools (inference based on absence of evidence).

### Gaps
- Bonfire baseball tee sleeve-vs-body colour options not verified; Zazzle/Teespring not researched; Spreadshirt/Custom Ink 3D preview and per-part colour unconfirmed (Custom Ink page doesn't mention them).
- No sources on inside-neck-label colour, button colour, or ribbing colour choices.

## Embroidery vs print placement; mockup/preview features

### Takeaway
Embroidery placements are fewer and smaller than print: Printful offers left chest, centre chest (both 4x4 in), large front (10x6 in) and sleeves; polos are embroidery only on Printful. Mockup features found are basic (flip front/back, pricing).

### Cited Findings
- See Printful numbers above — [Printful placement guide](https://www.printful.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide)
- Printful suggested left chest: ~3 in below neckline, ~2 in from armpit. — [Printful CA guide](https://www.printful.com/ca/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide) (via search summary)
- Custom Ink includes digital proofs and free art clean-up with orders; Group Order tools. — [Custom Ink FAQ](https://www.customink.com/custom/custom-clothing)
- Printify Product Creator lists print file requirements per provider and shows print areas per provider. — [Printify help](https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/4483637776401) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- A 4x4 in embroidery cap on left/centre chest is a sensible default for an embroidered placement in a studio.

### Gaps
- No confirmed info on 3D/mockup generator features of Printful/Printify/Spreadshirt (Printful mockup generator exists but its page wasn't retrieved); Printify embroidery size limits not found; Zazzle/Teespring not covered.
