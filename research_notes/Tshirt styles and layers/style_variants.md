## Which style variants are most commonly sold (rank by prevalence/popularity)?

### Takeaway
No sales-ranked data was found; sources are vendor blogs. The consistent picture is that the unisex crew jersey tee (Bella+Canvas 3001, Gildan 5000, Comfort Colors 1717) is the dominant base, with V-neck, long sleeve, raglan/baseball, tank, ringer, and boxy/heavyweight as the main variants.

## Per style: extra zones and how platforms let users colour them

### Takeaway
Only Custom Ink data on raglan/ringer was partially retrievable: contrast zones are sold as fixed factory colourways (e.g. White/Cardinal), not free per-part colour pickers. Per-part free colouring is a differentiator a studio would have to build.

### Cited Findings
- Custom Ink Canvas Ringer T: 5.0 oz 100% combed ringspun cotton jersey, solid body with contrast neck trim, slightly fitted, colours listed White and Black; page invites a note to coordinate ink colour to the ringer; S–2XL; MOQ 6. — [Custom Ink ringer](https://www.customink.com/products/styles/canvas-ringer-t/124400)
- Custom Ink Bella+Canvas Long Sleeve Raglan: 4.2 oz, contrast raglan sleeves and neck trim, full-length sleeves with ribbed cuffs, straight non-curved hem, semi-fitted ("skims body, chest, arms"); shown as White/Cardinal, "more than half a dozen colour combinations"; S–2XL for the shown colour. — [Custom Ink LS raglan](https://www.customink.com/products/styles/bella-+-canvas-long-sleeve-raglan-t-shirt/124500)
- Custom Ink baseball raglan: contrast sleeves and neck trim, more than half a dozen combinations; Juniors cap-sleeve raglan: contrast cap sleeves and neck binding. [search-summary only] — [Custom Ink search results above]
- Custom Ink warns deep shirt colours (cardinal, maroon, forest) can distort ink colour; team may call with substitutions. [search-summary only] — [Custom Ink help](https://customink.com/help_center/choosing-the-best-ink-colors)
- Colourway selection mechanism in Custom Ink Design Lab for contrast parts: page does not explain it. — [Custom Ink ringer/raglan](https://www.customink.com/custom/custom-ringer-raglan-t-shirts)
- Style definitions (retail blogs): ringer = contrast trim on collar and sleeve cuffs; raglan = seam from collar to underarm, wider chest for large graphics; baseball tee = contrast raglan sleeves + crew neck; henley = button placket; hooded tee = hood on a light tee. [search-summary only] — [PrintKK](https://www.printkk.com/blog/articles/types-of-t-shirts)

### Inferences (partly general knowledge, unsourced)
Zone model by style [general knowledge, verify]:
- Crew / V / scoop / long sleeve / tank: body, sleeves (if any), neck binding (usually same colour as body; tipping/contrast binding only on specialty blanks).
- Raglan / baseball (short, 3/4, long): body, left+right raglan sleeve (separately colourable as one panel each), neck binding often follows sleeve colour (per Custom Ink description), cuff/hem usually follow sleeve colour.
- Ringer: body, collar band, left+right sleeve hem bands (colour usually shared).
- Henley: body, placket (+ buttons, a colour of their own).
- Polo: body, collar, placket, buttons, cuffs.
- Hooded tee: body, hood (outer), hood lining, optional kangaroo pocket, drawcord; cuffs.
- Pocket tee: body plus a chest pocket patch (same fabric colour; pocket is also a print/no-print zone).
- Boxy/oversized/drop shoulder: same zones as crew; geometry differs (below).
- Recommended studio data model: each style = list of named colourable parts with default linkage (e.g. cuffs linked to sleeves), mirrored left/right.

## Print areas offered per style and sizing/fit effects on print geometry

### Takeaway
Front/back/sleeve zones are standard; max front sizes vary by product and size (up to 15x18 in on some DTG products), and youth/small sizes use smaller boxes. Women's/fitted cuts are narrower, so same-position artwork must scale.

### Cited Findings
- Custom Ink decoration areas: Front, Back, Left Sleeve, Right Sleeve (screen or digital print). — [Custom Ink](https://www.customink.com/custom/custom-ringer-raglan-t-shirts)
- Printify zone guide: left chest 2.5-5 in (4x4 standard adult; ~3 in below shoulder seam); centre chest 6x6 to 10x8 (8x8 common), youth typically 7x7 or smaller; full front 10-12 W x 10-14 H, 3-4 in below collar; oversized front 12-15 W x 14-16 H, some providers up to 15x18 on larger adult tees; collar/small upper back 1-3 in square about 1 in below collar; upper back 10-14 W x 1-6 H; full back 10-14 W x 6-15 H; sleeve 1-4 in standard (1-3 in above hem, at least 1 in from seams), up to 20x12 on all-over print; no neck-label zone covered. Printify product creator scales designs proportionally for smaller sizes. — [Printify placement guide](https://printify.com/blog/t-shirt-design-placement-guide/)
- Printful has a "standard 15x18" DTG print placement currently on certain sizes (L–4XL) of Bella+Canvas 3413 tri-blend and 3001. [search-summary only] — [Printful help](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/18532732956828)
- Printify Bella+Canvas 3001 front and back print areas are each 2752x3142 px per a third-party catalogue mirror (verify in Printify editor). [search-summary only] — [listingview mirror](https://listingview.io/products/unisex-jersey-short-sleeve-tee-12-1819)
- Printify raglan listings: 3/4 raglan shows 4 print areas (Tultex) / another listing 3. [search-summary only] — see Printify links above.
- Bella+Canvas 3001 spec: chest widths 16.5-32 in across sizes, 4.2 oz Airlume, side seams, shoulder taping; XS–3XL widths about 16.5-28 in, lengths 27-33 in. — [Blankstyle spec](https://www.blankstyle.com/files/specs/492/SpecSheetMeasurements_BC3001.pdf), [Blue Cotton](https://www.bluecotton.com/blog/?p=8577) [search-summary only]
- Bella+Canvas 3200 3/4 baseball tee: XS–2XL, widths 18-27.25 in, lengths 25.5-30.9 in. [search-summary only] — [Blue Cotton](https://www.bluecotton.com/blog/?p=8577)
- Boxy tees: Bonfire Comfort Colors Boxy is "slightly cropped", relaxed boxy fit; other retailers describe oversized boxy with dropped shoulders. [search-summary only] — [Bonfire boxy](https://www.bonfire.com/catalog/comfort-colors-boxy-t-shirt), [FLYP](https://flyp.space/products/oversized-boxy-tshirt)
- Spreadshirt kids organic tee sizes 2-8 years; teen and toddler variants exist. [search-summary only] — [Spreadshop](https://www.spreadshop.com/products/detail/kids-premium-organic-t-shirt/1370/eu)
- Custom Ink: women's fitted cuts (Bella+Canvas, Next Level, Comfort Colors) and youth sizing exist. — [Custom Ink](https://www.customink.com/custom/custom-ringer-raglan-t-shirts)

### Inferences
- Print-area geometry should be parameterised per style x fit x size: chest width, shoulder drop (boxy/drop-shoulder move the sleeve seam outward and lengthen sleeves visually), body length (cropped shorter, so full-front height box shrinks), neck drop (V/scoop move the top of the print box lower), and sleeve length/taper (tank has no sleeve zone; 3/4 and long sleeves allow larger sleeve boxes). Raglan seams cross the chest-top diagonally, so a "full front" box must clear the raglan seam, unlike set-in sleeves. [reasoning, not directly sourced]
- Youth: use ~7x7 in centre chest cap per Printify; toddler smaller.

### Gaps
- No Printful per-placement dimensions fetched; no Spreadshirt, Zazzle, Bonfire print-area dimensions; no pocket tee, hooded tee, henley, polo, tank print areas; women's vs unisex print-area differences not quantified anywhere found.

## Mockup photo conventions per platform

### Takeaway
Little platform-specific evidence was found. Only Custom Ink's page structure (front default image plus five supporting images) was confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Custom Ink style pages show a default front image, a catalogue detail image and five supporting images (contents not described). — [Custom Ink ringer](https://www.customink.com/products/styles/canvas-ringer-t/124400), [LS raglan](https://www.customink.com/products/styles/bella-+-canvas-long-sleeve-raglan-t-shirt/124500)

[search-summary only] — [Photoroom](https://docs.photoroom.com/tutorials/how-to-create-listing-images-for-clothing-and-apparel), [graswald](https://www.graswald.ai/blog/ai-packshots-for-fashion-how-to-create-flat-lay-and-ghost-mannequin-images-without-a-separate-shoot)

### Inferences
- A studio should render at least front and back flat views per style plus left/right sleeve views for sleeve-printable styles; ghost mannequin/flat is the common blank-garment convention. [general knowledge, unverified for each platform]
